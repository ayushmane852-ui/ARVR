# ?? Aesthetic Archetypes & Color Tokens

Use these ready-to-deploy design archetypes to prevent generic 'AI slop' UI.

---

## Archetype 1: Clean Editorial / Modern Journal
- **Best For**: Blogs, newsletters, boutique agencies, storytelling sites, essays, high-end portfolios.
- **Font Pairing**: 
  - Headings: *Fraunces* or *Playfair Display* (Google Fonts)
  - Body: *Plus Jakarta Sans* or *Inter* (Google Fonts)
- **Palette Tokens**:
  - Background: #FBFBF9 (warm paper)
  - Foreground: #121212 (rich ink)
  - Muted: #F0EDE6
  - Accent / Primary: #C25E38 (warm terracotta) or #2A4B3C (deep evergreen)
  - Border: #E6E2D8
- **Design Signatures**: Generous whitespace, elegant hairline rules, multi-column editorial layout, drop-caps or large lead paragraphs.

---

## Archetype 2: Modern Precision Tech / Bento SaaS
- **Best For**: Developer tools, SaaS dashboards, AI startups, technical platforms.
- **Font Pairing**:
  - Headings: *Geist Sans* or *Space Grotesk*
  - Body: *Geist Sans* or *Inter*
- **Palette Tokens (Dark Mode Preferred)**:
  - Background: #09090B (zinc 950)
  - Card: #121215 (zinc 900)
  - Foreground: #FAFAFA
  - Border: #27272A (zinc 800)
  - Accent: #3B82F6 (electric cobalt) or #10B981 (emerald)
- **Design Signatures**: Bento grid cards (grid grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-3 gap-4), 1px borders, subtle radial top spotlight (g-[radial-gradient(ellipse_80%_80%_at_50%_-20%,rgba(120,119,198,0.15),rgba(255,255,255,0))]), monospace metadata badges.

---

## Archetype 3: Neo-Brutalist
- **Best For**: Creative portfolios, modern web3, bold startups, trendy retail, Gen-Z brands.
- **Font Pairing**:
  - Headings: *Syne* or *Clash Display*
  - Body: *Space Mono* or *JetBrains Mono*
- **Palette Tokens**:
  - Background: #FFFDF5 (warm cream) or #FFFFFF
  - Foreground: #000000 (stark black)
  - Border: #000000 (thick 2px or 3px solid black)
  - Accents: #FFE600 (acid yellow), #00F5D4 (neo mint), #FF5E7E (hot pink)
- **Design Signatures**: Hard offset drop shadows (shadow-[4px_4px_0px_0px_#000]), high-contrast pill tags, unapologetic bold typography.

---

## Archetype 4: Craft / Luxury
- **Best For**: High-end fashion, architectural studios, luxury goods, fine dining.
- **Font Pairing**:
  - Headings: *Cinzel* or *Cormorant Garamond*
  - Body: *Outfit* or *General Sans*
- **Palette Tokens**:
  - Background: #0D0E12 (midnight obsidian)
  - Foreground: #F4F4F5
  - Accent: #D4AF37 (champagne gold) or #BFA181 (warm bronze)
  - Border: #1F222E
- **Design Signatures**: Glassmorphism (ackdrop-blur-md bg-white/[0.03]), fine hairline borders, subtle ambient glow, slow deliberate transitions.
